Maelstrom Harasses Valentino

Deep within the territory of the Valentino’s there is a place where the Burrito’s are hot, the Churro’s are sweet, and the atmosphere is as preem as the food. El Pinche Pollo will always have a place in my heart as the first place I tasted Adobo Burrito’s, smothered in sauce. Unfortunately however, a rogue Maelstrom Bunny has decided to grace the rooftops with her presence and rain various hacks from above. Victims report her ability to go invisible has them vexed and concerned.

Shadow Ripper

Stalking the night there appears to be a Shadow Ripper afoot, turning respectable mercs into cat-themed exotics. While the changes are easily reversed the sudden uptick in cases of involuntary cat people has certain circles concerned. One afflicted resident, a Merc named Victor agreed to the following interview.
