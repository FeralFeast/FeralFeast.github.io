Shadow Ripper

Stalking the night there appears to be a Shadow Ripper afoot, turning respectable mercs into cat-themed exotics. While the changes are easily reversed the sudden uptick in cases of involuntary cat people has certain circles concerned. One afflicted resident, a Merc named Victor agreed to the following interview.

How do you feel about your newfound appendages?

“Hate my new appendages cause they're not what I wanted, the doc fucked up and put 'em too far forward on my head. And this shit was done against my will because I got knocked out beforehand.”

What’s your opinion of Exotics?

“I do love exotics, it's been cool seeing so many in the city, despite how shit this place is it's nice to see people being able to express themselves.”

Has your recent involuntary physical change shifted your opinion on exotics?

“Nah my opinion hasn't changed cause of one nutjob, honestly if I were to willingly get exotics parts I would have gotten wolf parts instead of cat. That's just me, though.”
